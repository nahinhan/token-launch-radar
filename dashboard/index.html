How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth

  • TVL — the protocol's size today, in whole dollars.
  • Growth — its 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%, weighted 0.6 (never below 0.55×). A week above +100% is a one-off spike, not momentum, so the model stops rewarding it there.
  • Maturity — 0.55 + 0.45 × days listed (capped at 4 years): the longer it has run with no token, the more the wait is worth.
  • Breadth — +2% per extra chain, up to 5: a wider distribution surface.

An implied value is the radar's own estimate from the snapshot's figures — never a prediction that a token will launch, or when. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. radar-3 tightens the growth clamp from +150% to +100%; everything else is the radar-2 formula.
